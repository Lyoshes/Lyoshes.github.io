Elemental Blocks Privacy Policy

Developer: TeamRespawn
Privacy/Support contact: teamrespawn.dev@gmail.com
Effective date: 2026-09-06

Data and its use

Elemental Blocks stores progress, scores, unlocked features, discovered combinations, preferences and diagnostic gameplay runs locally on your device. This enables offline play and troubleshooting. Local diagnostics include installation, session and run identifiers, app version, timestamps, mode, level, results and gameplay events. No account or login is currently required.

Firebase Analytics is enabled in the current internal testing build to understand usage and improve game balance and reliability. It sends aggregated session and gameplay events, mode, level, score, duration, moves, outcomes, app version, platform and build markers to Google. Google's SDK also processes analytics identifiers and technical device/browser information and may use cookies or device storage. Advertising signals are disabled by this integration.

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any diagnostic files you choose to provide, solely to respond and investigate your request. Please do not send passwords or account tokens.

Prepared services — currently disabled

Firebase Anonymous Authentication (account identifier), Cloud Firestore (selected progress, records, unlocks and collection), Android Crashlytics (crash and device diagnostics), and App Check with web reCAPTCHA Enterprise or Android Play Integrity (app/device attestation for backend protection) are prepared but disabled. They do not currently collect data through this integration. This policy will be updated before those features are activated.

Service providers and security

When enabled, Firebase features share the data described above with Google to provide analytics, cloud functionality or security. Network requests to Firebase use HTTPS. We do not sell app data. See Firebase privacy information and Google's Privacy Policy.

The game remains on GitHub Pages. These Privacy and Support pages use Firebase Hosting and contain no game or Analytics scripts. Hosting providers process technical request information, such as IP addresses, to serve and secure their services. See GitHub's Privacy Statement for game hosting.

Retention and deletion

Local progress and preferences remain until you clear the app's storage or the game's browser site data. Local diagnostic history is bounded and older runs are replaced as new runs are saved. Settings → Diagnostics lets you download or clear local analytics; this does not delete progress or the local installation identifier. Clearing all app/site data removes these local identifiers and saves. Files you previously downloaded must be deleted separately.

Analytics data held by Google is retained according to the Firebase/Google Analytics property's retention settings and Google's applicable policies; clearing local storage does not delete previously transmitted data. Support correspondence is kept only as long as needed to handle the request, unless further retention is required by law. To request deletion of support information or identifiable analytics data, contact teamrespawn.dev@gmail.com. We may need information to locate the relevant records. Aggregated data that cannot be linked to you may not be individually identifiable for deletion.
